Privacy Policy — igo Coach

Effective date: October 16, 2026

igo Coach ("the app") is a Go teaching app for Android, published by naomi.jpn ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your games, ratings and settings stay on your device. We run no server and never receive them.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure those ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, personalized ads are shown only with your consent, which you can change at any time in Settings → Privacy options. In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of personalized ads in the same place.
  • No account, no analytics, no crash reporting. Playing, analyzing and learning all work fully offline.

Who is responsible

naomi.jpn Tokyo, Japan Email: nomi.ai.jpn@gmail.com

Data that stays on your device

The app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Finished games: the moves, the final score, and the date and time. Games you import are stored the same way.
  • Your rating history: your rating before and after each game against the AI, which AI generation you played, and the date and time.
  • Settings: your chosen language and which introductory tours you have already seen.

This data is never sent to us or to any third party by the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off in Android's settings.

Sharing a game

When you tap Export on a game, the app creates a file containing that game's moves and score and hands it to Android's share menu. Where the file goes is your choice. When you import a game, the app reads only the file you select.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner on the home screen and, after some finished games, a full-screen ad. Ads are provided by Google (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere) through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve ads, the SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, and where available, Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features (Topics and Attribution);
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, Android version, app version and language;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, to personalize them where you have allowed it, to measure their performance, and to prevent fraud.

Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. The legal basis for personalized advertising is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options; the new choice applies from the next time you start the app. If you don't consent, you may see non-personalized ads or no ads.

US state privacy laws. Some US states, such as California, give you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. We never receive or sell your data ourselves, but letting Google use the data above to personalize ads can count as "selling" or "sharing" under those laws. If you live in one of these states, open Settings → Privacy options and choose not to sell or share your personal information; the app then asks Google to restrict how it processes your data, so you see non-personalized ads.

Other controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Without an internet connection, no ads load, and everything else in the app keeps working.

More about how Google handles this data:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Play statistics

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google processes information about app downloads, installation and use under its own privacy policy, and gives us only aggregated statistics in the Play Console — for example install counts, app versions, device types, countries, and technical performance reports. We use them to keep the app working and to improve it, never to identify individual users.

Emailing us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and to look into the issue you report, keep it only as long as that takes (or as long as the law requires), and delete it on request. We are based in Japan, so your message is received and handled there.

What the app does not do

The app has no account or sign-in, no analytics, and no crash reporting. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files beyond one you pick to import. We do not sell your data for money or give it to data brokers (for how US state laws treat personalized ads, see "US state privacy laws" above).

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The app itself collects no personal data from anyone, children included. If you are a parent or guardian with a question, contact us at nomi.ai.jpn@gmail.com.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that happened before. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to have it corrected or deleted, to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising, and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

Apart from any email you send us, we hold no personal data about you: the data on your device is under your control and can be deleted as described above. For advertising data, you can use the controls described above or contact Google. For anything else, contact us at nomi.ai.jpn@gmail.com.
